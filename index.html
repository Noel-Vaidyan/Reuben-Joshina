How It All Began

Our Story

♥
"

It started with simple hellos exchanged across a desk, the kind of conversations neither of us knew would change our lives forever. What began as casual chats soon turned into shared lunches, endless conversations, and countless memories made together. We discovered a love for exploring new places, trying new foods, and finding joy in the little moments that made every day feel special.

As life took us down different paths, we faced three years of long distance. Miles separated us, time zones challenged us, and there were days when all we had were phone calls and messages. Yet through every goodbye, every late-night conversation, and every countdown to seeing each other again, our love only grew stronger. Distance never weakened what we had — it taught us patience, trust, commitment, and the true meaning of choosing each other every single day.

Five years later, we've shared adventures across cities, celebrated milestones, overcome challenges, and created a lifetime of memories together. We've been each other's biggest supporters, trusted confidants, travel companions, and partners through every chapter of life. What started with a simple hello has grown into a love story built on friendship, laughter, resilience, and unwavering commitment.

Today, we're not just partners in travel, food, and adventure. We're partners in every sense of the word — ready to continue writing this beautiful story together for the rest of our lives.
